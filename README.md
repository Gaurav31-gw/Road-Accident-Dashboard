# 🚦 Road Accident Dashboard  

## 📊 Project Overview  
This project presents a **Road Accident Dashboard** built in **Tableau**, designed to analyze and visualize road accident data.  
The dashboard highlights accident trends, casualties, severity, and contributing factors such as weather, road surface, and location.  

The aim is to provide an **interactive and insightful representation** of road accident data for better decision-making and road safety awareness.

## 🔑 Key Insights  
- **Total Accidents**: 1,44,419 (↓ 11.70% YoY)  
- **Total Casualties**: 1,95,737 (↓ 11.89% YoY)  
- **Fatal Casualties**: 2,855 (↓ 26.40% YoY)  
- **Casualty Breakdown**: Serious (27,045), Slight (1,65,837)  

**Factors Analyzed:**  
- Fatal Casualties by Vehicle Type (Cars, buses, bicycles, motorcycles, etc.)  
- Weather conditions (Rain, Snow/Fog, Fine, etc.)  
- Road surface (Single carriageway, dual carriageway, etc.)  
- Location distribution (Map visualization of accident hotspots).  

## ❓ KPI Questions  
- What is the YoY trend in **total accidents**?  
- Which **vehicle type** contributes most to fatal casualties?  
- How do **weather conditions** affect fatal accident rates?  
- Which **road surface type** records the highest fatal accidents?  
- What is the **geographic distribution** of fatal casualties?  
- How do **serious vs slight casualties** compare across years?  
- Which conditions (road/weather/vehicle) contribute to the **highest risk factors**?  

## 🎯 Project Outcome  
- Built an **interactive Tableau Dashboard** analyzing road accidents.  
- Identified **critical KPIs**: Total Accidents, Total Casualties, Fatal, Serious & Slight Casualties.  
- Provided **filters for year comparison & accident severity**, making dashboard dynamic.  
- Enabled **data-driven decision making** for road safety improvements.  

## 💡 Project Insights  
- **Total accidents and casualties** have decreased YoY, which is a positive safety indicator.  
- **Cars and motorcycles** contribute significantly to fatal casualties compared to other vehicle types.  
- **Weather conditions** (Rain, Snow/Fog) play a smaller role, with most fatalities happening in **fine weather** (85%).  
- **Single carriageway roads** are the most dangerous, accounting for ~73% of fatal casualties.  
- **Geographical clustering** shows higher accident density in urban and southern regions.
